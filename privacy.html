Laney — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Laney may process when used in Discord servers.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled in a server, Laney may process Discord information such as user IDs, usernames, server IDs, channel IDs, message content, and timestamps.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide Laney's features, including welcome messages, introduction checking, message management, reactions, and server functionality.

3. Data Storage

Laney may temporarily store limited information required for certain features to function. Information is not sold or used for advertising.

4. Third Parties

Laney does not sell personal information to third parties. Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service also apply when using Discord.

5. Data Requests

If you have questions about information processed by Laney, please contact the bot developer through the Discord community where Laney is being used.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The latest version will be published on this page.
